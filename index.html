About me

I turn messy problems into simple, human-centered solutions using data, design and product thinking.

Work that makes a real difference for people is what drives me.

  1. 01

    I make complicated things simple and beautiful, whether it's a classroom lesson for young kids or a handoff guide for a nonprofit team to use.

  2. 02

    I can take an idea from research to real: talking to users, finding the patterns, and designing and testing until it works.

  3. 03

    I study how people live with AI. I designed Kidvisor for children's digital well-being, I'm on a systematic review team researching how families learn with AI, and I analyzed survey data from 25 countries on how people trust AI.

Outside of design, find me enjoying fall pastries, making mood boards and brainstorming my next project.
