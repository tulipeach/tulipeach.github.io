About me

A little about me

I'm an empathetic, hands-on designer who keeps people at the center of everything I make. I love building human-centered, ethical technology, and I'm drawn to work that sits between technology, education and community.

Outside of class, I teach Islamic studies to kids in my mosque community, do PR for MSA Volunteer Corps, and volunteer whenever I can. I care deeply about social justice, human rights and the environment.

I love rainy days, fall, mood boards, coloring, making cute desserts, and making messy things simple and beautiful.
