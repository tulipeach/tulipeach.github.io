About me

A little about me

I moved to the United States as a child and changed homes and schools more times than I can count. It taught me to adapt quickly, and to notice the people who are still finding their footing.

Today I study Informatics at the University of Washington, teach at a weekend school, and spend my free time on community work. I love rainy days, fall, a good book, and making messy things simple and beautiful.
